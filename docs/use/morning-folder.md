# The Morning folder

The small folder on your screen is where things that need you wait: one card each, in folders. Open it whenever you
like. Reading a card never decides anything for you.

## Cards

A card says what something is, what it means for you, and what you can do about it. Cards come from three places:

- **Your team's jobs.** A job that found something puts one card in a folder named after it, such as "Pages up · 3:12 PM:
  1 of 3 pages need a look". It shows what the job found, its files, and **Open job**.
- **Jobs that read for you.** When a job reads your mail or a page, what matters becomes cards with a short title, one
  sentence on what it means for you, and up to three options, best first.
- **You.** **Create a note** in the **⋯** menu, or **New note** on the home, keeps your own card.

Cards carry forward from day to day. When the same thing turns up again, its card is updated rather than repeated.

## Deciding what to do

Open a card, then pick one:

- **An option**, such as "Draft a reply", hands the work to Noteling. You follow it in the task window and can stop it
  any time.
- **I'll do it** keeps the card under **I'll handle it**.
- **Ignore** files it away.
- **I've handled this** marks it resolved. **Reopen this card** brings it back.

A card from your team's tools has its own buttons instead. They open a page, ask Noteling about the card in chat, or
open the job's page. A card a job wrote can also have a button that does something about it, such as **Mark as fixed**:
pressing it runs that job's own step, as you, and the card says what came of it. A button that changes something asks
first. Such a card never makes Noteling do anything by itself.

In the card's **⋯** menu, **Discuss or adjust** opens a chat about it, to ask a question or explain what matters to you.
Talking about a card doesn't start any work unless you ask.

The picker at the top (**To review**, **Filed away**, **I'll handle it**, **With Noteling**, **Result ready**,
**Resolved**) shows the cards where you put them.

## Several at once

Open a folder and choose **Select**, or ⌘-click a card. Tick the cards you want, then choose what to do with all of them:
**Resolve**, **File away** or **I'll do it** (other views offer **Back to review** or **Reopen**). **Select all** takes every
card the folder shows, and one **Undo** puts them all back. A card with work under way stays as it is.

## Files on a card

Some cards carry files, such as a table of everything a job checked. On a job's card, the short tables and notes it
marks as a preview show right on the card. Every file has **Open**, **Save as…** and **Show in Finder**. To change one,
in Excel say, use **Save as…** first: the next run replaces the files.

## Keeping the home tidy

- **Quiet folders** step aside. A folder with nothing to show folds into **Quiet folders · Show** at the bottom of the
  home, and comes back by itself when a card arrives.
- **Hide a folder** you don't need: right-click it, or open it, and choose **Hide folder**. Nothing in it is deleted, and
  new cards still arrive. **Hidden folders · Show** brings it back.
- **Hide the Morning folder itself**: right-click it and choose **Hide Morning folder**, or use **Hide Morning Folder** in
  the menu bar. **Morning Files…** in the menu bar still opens it.

## Who's Who

**Who's Who** in the **⋯** menu keeps the people you work with: their role and how you work together. Noteling uses it
when it prepares work from a card.
