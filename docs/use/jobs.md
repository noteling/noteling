# Your team's jobs

A job is a small program someone on your team wrote to check something for you, on a schedule: the prices on a list of
items during a sale, orders stuck for more than a day, pages that should be up. It runs on your Mac, as you, and only
takes your attention when it finds something.

Open **Jobs** from the top of the Morning folder's home, or **Jobs…** in the menu bar.

## Turn on the ones that are yours

Your team's jobs are listed under "From your team's tools", each with a switch. Turn on the ones that are yours: only
those run on your Mac and tell you anything. A job you turn on runs right away.

When your team has many jobs, a box above them finds yours. Type part of a name or a folder, such as `gm` or
`holiday oct`, and only the matching jobs show. Noteling keeps your search until you choose **Clear**, and the line under
the box says how many it shows, such as "Showing 5 of 100". Jobs you've turned on always show, whatever you typed.

You can also ask in chat: "turn on gm", "what jobs do I have?".

**Mail**, under Your jobs, is Noteling's own: it reads your unread mail of the last two days, once `MAIL_ADDRESS` and
`MAIL_APP_PASSWORD` are in Settings ([how](reading-jobs.md)). A job that needs a secret you haven't entered waits, and
its row says so.

## Each job, at a glance

A job's row says:

- its name and folder, and who shared it ("Shared by Yang");
- what it's for, and how often it runs ("Every hour", or "When you run it");
- how its last run went, such as "Last run 9:02 AM · 2 of 8 prices wrong", in red when it failed, with why.

**Run now** runs it at once. When it's done, a line says what it found, with a word of thanks to whoever shared it.

## What it found

Click a job to open its page:

- **Input**: the files it reads, such as the list of items it checks.
- **Latest result**: what its last run that worked left. Short tables and notes are shown right there, as they are.
  Every file has **Open**, **Save as…** and **Show in Finder**, so you can take the full results into Excel.

## Its stickers, and when it tells you

A job reports what it found as rows. Noteling's minister reads what changed and decides which of it deserves your
attention now, as a sticky note beside Noteling's note on your screen: a sentence or two in plain words, the job's name,
who shared it and when. It shows only sure things: yours, needing you, true now, and new to you. Everything else stays
quiet in Noteling's memory, with the reason, and you can ask in chat: "anything about Acme?", "why did I get that sticker?",
"what did the orders job find yesterday?".

- Up to three stickers sit there, urgent ones first, and **and 2 more** above them opens Jobs. They step aside while the
  chat is open, and come back with it.
- Click a sticker to open it: the words, why you're seeing it, the rows behind it, the buttons the job offers (such as
  **Nudge the warehouse**, which asks first), and **Open page**.
- The small × that appears when you point at a sticker puts it away. It stays away until something about it changes and
  the minister thinks you should see it again.
- A sticker leaves by itself when the job no longer reports what it was about.
- An urgent sticker is also a notification. Clicking the notification opens the sticker.
- Drag a sticker to move the whole stack. Beside the note, the stickers go where the note goes; once you've put them
  somewhere else they stay there, whatever the note does, until **Put back beside Noteling** in their right-click menu.
- Right-click a sticker for **Open**, **Put away**, **Bigger** and **Smaller** (the size is remembered) and **Hide
  stickers**. Hidden stickers come back from **Show Stickers** in the menu bar, and when Noteling next starts; an urgent
  one still notifies you meanwhile.

## Before it starts, and after it ends

A job can have a start and an end, such as a sale that runs for four days. Before its start and after its end, it stays
out of your way: it doesn't run, doesn't notify you, and its cards are put away.

In Jobs, such jobs wait under one line at the bottom: **Upcoming (1) · Ended (2) · Show**. Click it to look at them. You
can turn on an upcoming job ahead of time, and it runs from its start. If your team moves an end later, the job and its
cards come back.

## When something's wrong

- **"Needs … in Settings."** The job needs a password or key from you. Choose **Open Settings**, and enter it under
  **Tool packs**. Noteling keeps it in your Mac's Keychain.
- **A red last run.** The line says why. If it says the same thing twice, tell whoever shared the job: the name is on
  its row.
- **"Your team's tools still have a watches/ folder."** Earlier versions checked lists of items called watches. They no
  longer run; whoever keeps your team's tools can move them to jobs.
