# The pen and chat

Two ways to ask Noteling about your work: point at something on your screen with the pen, or open chat and type.

## Point with the pen

1. Hold the note until its ring fills, or press **⌃⌥Space**. The pointer becomes a quill.
2. **Click** what you mean, or **drag** to circle it.
3. The pad asks "What about it?", with a few questions to tap. Tap one, type your own, or press **Return** for a short
   explanation. **Esc** puts the pick away.

Nothing goes to Claude until you ask. On pages your team's tools know, their answer is on the pad the moment you pick,
before anyone is asked: what's going on, why, and what to do.

The pen never takes your mouse or keyboard. It explains, and answers appear as they're written.

## Chat

Double-click the note, or click Noteling in the Dock. Each question becomes a sticky note with its answer written on it,
and follow-ups are tabs under it.

Chat can also look after your jobs and cards: "what did my inbox check find?", "turn on gm", "run pages up now".

## What it looks at

- **Before you send**, a line over the box says whether your question will look at your screen, such as **Looks at
  "New Report"**, or **Doesn't look at your screen**. Click it to change that for this one question.
- **When it takes the screen**, it flashes, like a phone taking a screenshot, and the picture shrinks into the chat. It
  answers from the screen as it was when you asked, so you can move on while it works.
- **Under your question**, a small chip says what it was shown, such as "Saw your screen · 10:31". Click it to see
  exactly that.

## Letting it do things

Mouse and keyboard control is off until you turn it on, in Settings or with the hand button on the chat pad. When it's
on and you ask Noteling to do something, the task window shows its progress and any approval it needs, and **Stop**
ends it at any time.

## Show it how, with Watch Me

Show Noteling how you do something once, and it keeps the steps:

1. Choose **Watch Me** in the menu bar, or the eye on the chat pad. Do the task, then choose **Stop Watching**
   (or press ⌃⌥Space).
2. Name it in a few words, such as "Submit an expense", with anything to skip or watch out for.
3. Look over the steps it wrote. To change something, just type it. Then choose **Keep it**.

From then on, the pen and chat use those steps in that app or on that page. Ask "submit an expense for me", and with
control on, Noteling does it by following your steps. What Watch Me keeps is a tool in your tools folder, not a job: it
never runs by itself.

## Let it show you the way

Ask where something is ("idk where to find the agent tokens", "how do I get to the history?") and Noteling shows you on
your own screen instead of writing out steps:

1. The rest of the screen dims, a purple ring sits on the control to click, Noteling's arrow points at it, and a small
   note beside it says what to do and which step this is.
2. **You** click it. Noteling never clicks for you: doing it yourself is how you'll know the way next time. When the next
   page shows the next control, the ring moves there. A control further down the page is brought into view first.
3. On the last step, click it or choose **Got it**. **Esc**, or the × on the note, stops at any time.

The steps also show on the chat pad, ticked off as you go, with **Show again** once you're done.

It shows the ways someone showed it with Watch Me: whoever knows the way records it once, and from then on anyone asking
about that page can be shown. It can also ring a single control that's on your screen now. If no one has shown it the
way, it says so and gives its best steps in words.

## Sticky notes

While the pen is up, **right-click** a control to stick a note on it: a tip for the next time you're on that page. Notes
show as small stickers when the pen is up, and Noteling reads them when you ask about that control. They stay on your
Mac. Press **⌥ Option**
twice to show the notes on the current page.
