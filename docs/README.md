# Noteling's guides

Pick the one that sounds like you.

## Using Noteling

You have Noteling on your Mac, or you're about to.

- [Getting started](use/getting-started.md): install it, allow access, connect Claude, and link your team's tools.
- [The Morning folder](use/morning-folder.md): cards, and deciding what to do with them.
- [Your team's jobs](use/jobs.md): turning on the jobs that are yours, finding them, and reading what they found.
- [Jobs that read for you](use/reading-jobs.md): showing Noteling your mail, a calendar or a page, and teaching it what
  matters to you.
- [The pen and chat](use/pen-and-chat.md): asking about what's on your screen.

## Keeping a team's tools

You write scripts for your team, and want them running on everyone's Mac and landing in front of the right people.

- [Start here](team/README.md): what a team repository is, and what goes in it.
- [Your first job](team/first-job.md): from nothing to a card on your screen in ten minutes.
- [Jobs](team/jobs.md): everything a job can do.
- [Tool packs](team/packs.md): tools for the chat and the pen on your team's apps and pages.
- [Cards](team/cards.md): the card file any script can write.
- [Setting up your team](team/setup.md): the token, setup files, secrets, and your company's Claude gateway.
- [Testing, and what each message means](team/testing.md)
- [Examples](team/examples.md): four patterns to copy.

The [template repository](https://github.com/noteling/team-tools-template) is a working team repository to start from.
