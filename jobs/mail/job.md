Each row is a message in the inbox that is still unread and arrived in the last two days, newest first: who it is from,
its subject, when it arrived, the first 300 characters of its text, and for Gmail the tab it landed in (primary,
social, promotions, updates, forums) and whether Gmail marked it important. `bulk` is yes for a newsletter or a mailing
list. A message the person reads, or one older than two days, is no longer a row.

The person's own inbox, read with their own app password; nothing is marked read, moved or deleted. Bulk mail and the
promotions, social and updates tabs are rarely worth attention; a message from a person who asks the reader for
something, or names a time, often is.
